Good to know

The quick questions.

Does it need special tissues?

No. Standard facial tissues fit. Our refill packs are folded to bloom into the leaf shape, so they look their best, but you are never locked in.

Is it hygienic?

Yes. Tissues sit in an enclosed chamber under the lid. You only ever touch the one you pull, exactly like a regular dispenser.

How do I refill it?

Lift the top, drop in a fresh stack of about 40, and set the lid back. That is the whole thing. Takes ten seconds.

What is the drooping leaf?

A hidden mechanism quietly lowers one leaf as the tissues run low. No batteries, no lights, no beeping. The plant simply looks like it could use some attention.
